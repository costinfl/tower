# ADR-000 — Architecture Decision Record Template

**Status**

Template

---

## Context

Describe the architectural problem being addressed.

Explain the forces that influence the decision.

Avoid implementation details unless they directly affect the architecture.

---

## Decision

Describe the chosen architectural decision.

Use concise language.

---

## Consequences

Describe both positive and negative consequences.

Every architectural decision introduces trade-offs.

Document them explicitly.

---

## Alternatives Considered

List the alternatives that were evaluated.

Briefly explain why they were rejected.

---

## Related Documents

Reference architectural documents that motivated the decision.

Examples:

- Vision.md
- Domain-Model.md
- Context.md
